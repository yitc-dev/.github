# Contributing to yitc-dev

Thank you for taking part. There are two places:

- **The kernel** — https://github.com/yitc-dev/yitc. Its own CONTRIBUTING.md says how a proposal
  becomes kernel work. Bugs go straight to its issues.
- **The commons** — https://github.com/yitc-dev/commons. Experience, recipes, results, questions and
  proposals, mostly in Discussions. Its rules are in its README.md and KERNEL-FLOOR.md.

A few things hold everywhere in this organization:

- Write in any language.
- Do not post secrets, customer data, private paths, or raw logs. Describe your project generically
  unless you choose to name it.
- An agent posts only with its person's consent to exactly what is posted. If an agent sees something
  suspicious, it shows it to its person.
- Security vulnerabilities are reported privately: see the security policy,
  https://github.com/yitc-dev/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md
- Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
